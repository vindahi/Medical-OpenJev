METHOD

A gate around your LLM, not a head after it.

Medical-OpenJev is a System-1 reflex for medical pipelines. One bidirectional forward pass returns routed specialty, evidence-sufficiency probability, calibrated confidence, and an act or escalate decision. It never generates diagnostic text.

Model

A frozen ModernBERT-base backbone with lightweight trained heads for evidence sufficiency and specialty routing.

Objective

RLCD with composite strictly proper scoring rewards trains the model to report honest probabilities.

Output

Routed specialty, evidence sufficiency, calibrated confidence, and an operational act or escalate decision.

RESULTS

Calibrated across three condition tiers.

Gate confidence versus a doctor LLM grading its own confidence, on 60 held-out cases per condition tier. Lower expected calibration error (ECE) is better.

Condition tierGate ECE ↓LLM ECE ↓
Dermatology (derm)0.2090.523

The gate is approximately 2–3× better calibrated than the LLM's confidence on every tier.

MODELS

Three condition-specific models, one repository.

A difficulty gradient from well-specified skin disease to broad internal medicine and rare, low-prevalence conditions.

ModelCondition tierExample diagnoses
derm/Dermatology — specific, history-groundedHailey-Hailey disease, Conradi-Hünermann syndrome

APPLICATIONS

Features for medical LLM workflows.

  • Three decision primitiveschoice specialty routing, noul evidence sufficiency, and act/escalate gating.
  • Calibrated by designRLCD uses composite strictly proper scoring rewards for honest confidence estimates.
  • Multi-turn friendlyAccumulate consultation evidence turn by turn; each turn is one state snapshot.
  • Privacy-firstFully local and Apache-2.0 with no data egress.
  • LightweightA frozen ModernBERT-base backbone plus tiny trained heads; each tier is one approximately 300 MB model.
